NĀḌĪFace vitals · camera-based wellness scan

What it estimates

  • Heart rate
  • Respiratory rate
  • Heart rate variability (RMSSD, SDNN)
  • Stress index (indicative)
  • Signal quality, so you know how far to trust it

For a good scan

  • Face a window or lamp. Even, bright light on your face; no backlight.
  • It starts by itself once you're ready. Stay still and quiet for about 30 seconds.
  • Remove glasses/cap if they cover the forehead or cheeks.
  • Prop the phone on a stand or table at about arm's length. Holding it in your hand adds motion that swamps the pulse.
  • Sit back a little so your shoulders are in view. Breathing is read from their movement, like Google Fit does.

Who is being scanned?

Only scan someone with their OK. Not meant for babies. Names stay on this phone.

Wellness estimate only. Not a medical device and not for diagnosis. Video is processed on your phone and is never uploaded or stored.
